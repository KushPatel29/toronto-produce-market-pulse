Pricing: how often to reprice

Gross-margin dollars over a full cycle are within 1% across policies; the difference is risk. Recommended: monthly list for apples, bananas, mushrooms; band rule for avocados, carrots, garlic, kale, lemons, onions; weekly for the rest.
